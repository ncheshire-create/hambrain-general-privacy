# HamBrain General Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

HamBrain General is a study aid for the FCC General class amateur radio license exam, published by Zbyt Studios. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

## Data we collect

None. The app works entirely offline. Your quiz progress, streak, and mock exam scores are stored only on your own device and never leave it. Deleting the app or using Reset progress inside the app removes this data.

## Third parties

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party services, and it does not share any data with anyone.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Affiliation

HamBrain General is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FCC, ARRL, or NCVEC. Questions are taken from the public NCVEC General class (Element 3) question pool.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
